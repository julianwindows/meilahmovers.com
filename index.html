About Meilah

Built in Kampala, moving all of Uganda forward.

MEILAH MOVERS & LOGISTICS began with a simple frustration: too many moving days in this city end in broken furniture, missing boxes and vague promises. We set out to fix that — with trained crews, real equipment, and a dispatch team that actually answers the phone.

Today we run house and office relocations, cross-town deliveries and regional freight from Kololo to Kansanga, Entebbe Road to Jinja Highway, with the same standard every time: careful handling, honest pricing, and a truck that arrives when we said it would. Whether it's a single-room apartment in Ntinda or a full warehouse relocation for a growing business, our crews plan the route, protect the load, and stay in touch until the last box is placed.

Our Mission

To make moving and logistics in Uganda predictable, affordable and stress-free — for families and businesses alike.

Our Vision

To be East Africa's most trusted name in moving and freight, one safely delivered load at a time.
